Discovery Tile

A place that felt timeless.

Neuschwanstein looks like it was dreamed up for a fairytale, yet it’s rooted in the very real visions of King Ludwig II. Standing there, you can’t help but imagine the stories he carried—and the ones he hoped to leave behind.
